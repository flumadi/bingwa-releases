Bingwa privacy policy

Version 1 · 13 November 2026 · mathiasfridah2@gmail.com

Who we are

  • Bingwa is an app for agents who sell Safaricom bundles. "Bingwa" is responsible for the personal data the app handles (the data controller under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019).
  • If you are an agent, you are also responsible for your own customers' data that you collect with Bingwa. Tell your customers that their number is used to send their bundle and a receipt.
  • Questions, requests and complaints: mathiasfridah2@gmail.com.

What the app collects

  • Your account: name, email address and password. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication; Bingwa never stores them.
  • Your lines: business name, the line's phone number and the till number.
  • M-PESA payments: from messages sent by the trusted senders you set (normally MPESA), the transaction code, amount, payer's name and number, and time. Messages from other senders are ignored, except a customer's text asking for your price list (e.g. OFFERS), which is answered and not kept.
  • Customers: the numbers bundles are sent to, names shown by M-PESA, purchase history, numbers you block, and contacts you save or pick (only the one you pick).
  • Bundles sent: the USSD codes dialled and Safaricom's replies. For Advanced plans, the Accessibility service looks at the screen only while Bingwa is running or recording a USSD request, to read and answer the USSD menu. It is idle the rest of the time.
  • Airtime cards: only the last 4 digits and a one-way fingerprint of the PIN. The photo is read on the phone and deleted.
  • The phone: model, app version, a random ID for the phone, whether automation is running, airtime balance and the day's totals.

Why

  • To send the bundle a customer paid for, reply to them, and show you your sales, earnings and problems to sort out.
  • To let you watch and control your phones from the web portal and other phones, and to send a payment to another of your phones.
  • To keep your account secure (email verification, screen lock before Start and Stop).
  • The legal bases are performing the service you asked for and your consent, which you can withdraw by deleting your account.

Where it is kept

  • On the phone, in the app's private storage.
  • When you sign in: in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Authentication), run by Google, possibly in data centres outside Kenya with Google's safeguards. This holds your phones' status, recent sales with customer numbers, plans, contacts, clients, USSD history, the activity log and commands. Security rules let only your login read or change it.
  • Excel files you export go wherever you save or share them.

Who sees it

  • You, and anyone you let sign in to your account.
  • Google, which hosts the cloud data for us.
  • Safaricom, as part of each M-PESA payment, USSD request and SMS.
  • GitHub, which hosts app updates and the web portal, sees your internet address when the app checks for updates.
  • We do not sell or rent personal data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking tools.
  • We may disclose data if Kenyan law requires it.

How long

  • On the phone: until you delete it or uninstall the app. The USSD history keeps the last 500 runs.
  • In the cloud: only the latest records are shared, and they are replaced as new ones arrive. Everything is deleted when you delete your account.

Your rights

  • See your data: the app shows it all, and More → Export to Excel gives you a copy.
  • Correct it: edit plans, contacts, clients and your profile in the app.
  • Delete it: Profile & settings → Delete my account removes your login and all its cloud data. Uninstalling removes what is on the phone. Or write to mathiasfridah2@gmail.com.
  • Object or withdraw consent: stop using the app and delete your account.
  • Complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (www.odpc.go.ke).

Security

  • Data travels encrypted (HTTPS). Cloud data is protected by security rules that allow only its owner.
  • Start and Stop ask for the phone's own screen lock. Airtime PINs are never stored. Keep your phone locked and your password private.

Children

  • Bingwa is for adults running a business. It is not meant for anyone under 18.

Changes

  • If this policy changes, the app shows it again and asks you to accept it before you continue.
  • Version 1, updated 13 November 2026.

Sisi ni nani

  • Bingwa ni programu ya mawakala wanaouza vifurushi vya Safaricom. "Bingwa" inawajibika kwa data binafsi inayoshughulikiwa na programu (mdhibiti wa data chini ya Sheria ya Ulinzi wa Data ya Kenya, 2019).
  • Ikiwa wewe ni wakala, unawajibika pia kwa data ya wateja wako unayokusanya kwa Bingwa. Waambie wateja wako kwamba namba yao inatumika kuwatumia kifurushi na risiti.
  • Maswali, maombi na malalamiko: mathiasfridah2@gmail.com.

Programu inakusanya nini

  • Akaunti yako: jina, barua pepe na nenosiri. Manenosiri hushughulikiwa na Google Firebase Authentication; Bingwa haiyahifadhi kamwe.
  • Laini zako: jina la biashara, namba ya simu ya laini na namba ya till.
  • Malipo ya M-PESA: kutoka ujumbe uliotumwa na watumaji unaowaamini (kwa kawaida MPESA), msimbo wa muamala, kiasi, jina na namba ya aliyelipa, na wakati. Ujumbe kutoka kwa watumaji wengine hupuuzwa, isipokuwa ujumbe wa mteja akiomba orodha ya bei (mf. OFFERS), ambao hujibiwa na hauhifadhiwi.
  • Wateja: namba zinazotumiwa vifurushi, majina yanayoonyeshwa na M-PESA, historia ya ununuzi, namba unazozuia, na anwani unazohifadhi au kuchagua (ile tu unayochagua).
  • Vifurushi vilivyotumwa: misimbo ya USSD iliyopigwa na majibu ya Safaricom. Kwa vifurushi vya hatua kwa hatua, huduma ya Accessibility huangalia skrini tu wakati Bingwa inaendesha au kurekodi ombi la USSD, ili kusoma na kujibu menyu ya USSD. Wakati mwingine wote haifanyi kitu.
  • Kadi za airtime: tarakimu 4 za mwisho na alama ya njia moja ya PIN tu. Picha husomwa kwenye simu na kufutwa.
  • Simu: modeli, toleo la programu, kitambulisho cha nasibu cha simu, kama otomatiki inaendelea, salio la airtime na jumla za siku.

Kwa nini

  • Kutuma kifurushi alicholipia mteja, kumjibu, na kukuonyesha mauzo, mapato na matatizo ya kushughulikia.
  • Kukuwezesha kuona na kudhibiti simu zako kutoka tovuti na simu nyingine, na kutuma malipo kwa simu yako nyingine.
  • Kulinda akaunti yako (kuthibitisha barua pepe, kufuli ya skrini kabla ya Kuanza na Kusimamisha).
  • Misingi ya kisheria ni kutoa huduma uliyoiomba na ridhaa yako, ambayo unaweza kuiondoa kwa kufuta akaunti yako.

Hii huhifadhi hali ya simu zako, mauzo ya karibuni pamoja na namba za wateja, vifurushi, anwani, wateja, historia ya USSD, kumbukumbu ya shughuli na amri. Sheria za usalama huruhusu akaunti yako tu kuisoma au kuibadilisha.
